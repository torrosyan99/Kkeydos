About Consumer Affairs

Founded in 1998, Consumer Affairs is a platform that brings consumers and brands together. They aim to empower consumers to make informed shopping decisions by providing access to client reviews, news, and expert resources.

The challenge.

Consumer Affairs needed help with front-end development, UX/UI design, and QA. The aim was to work on site improvements, mobile development, user experience enhancements, and enforce QA best practices.

Consumer Affairs needed help with front-end development, UX/UI design, and QA.

The solution.

Through our staff augmentation engagement model, we provided senior LATAM tech talent. Our bilingual specialists all undergo a rigorous vetting process that assesses not only their technical expertise, but also their soft skills.

All technologies used.

  • MySQL
  • Django
  • Python
  • JavaScript
  • Selenium
  • HTML5

The outcome.

During our engagement we:

  • Worked on site maintenance, infrastructure, site improvements, and side applications that complemented the site and the new brands app.
  • Crafted websites, native mobile apps, and brand experiences that connected with millions of consumers.
  • Shaped UX/UI design and front-end development to prioritize usability and performance.
  • Provided comprehensive testing services, seamlessly integrating QA practices to align with and enhance business objectives.
