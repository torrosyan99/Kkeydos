General Questions

Kkeydos is a custom software development company that designs, develops, and maintains digital products for businesses. We build AI-powered applications, websites, mobile apps, SaaS platforms, ERP and CRM systems, e-commerce solutions, APIs, and custom enterprise software.

We work on new digital products, business automation systems, SaaS platforms, mobile applications, e-commerce platforms, enterprise applications, AI solutions, CRM/ERP systems, and modernization of existing software.

Yes. We can take a project from initial idea and requirements through product strategy, UI/UX design, development, testing, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.

Yes. We work with startups, small and medium-sized businesses, and established enterprises. The development approach, technology stack, and engagement model can be adjusted according to the project's stage, budget, and growth plans.

Our primary focus is custom software development. We build solutions around your business processes rather than forcing your requirements into a fixed software product.

Project Process

Our typical process includes:

Discovery → Requirements → UI/UX Design → Architecture → Development → QA & Testing → Deployment → Support

The exact process can be adapted depending on the project's complexity and engagement model.

We evaluate the project scope, features, user roles, integrations, UI/UX requirements, technology stack, security requirements, and expected scale. Based on this assessment, we provide an estimated timeline and development cost.

The timeline depends on the product's complexity. A small website or MVP may take several weeks, while a complex SaaS, ERP, CRM, AI platform, or enterprise application may require several months.

We provide a project-specific timeline after understanding the requirements.

Ownership & Security
